How it works

Ask. Understand. Decide. Fix.

CloudPilot is designed to keep you in control. It can investigate your infrastructure and explain what it finds before anything needs to change.

01

Connect AWS

Give CloudPilot access to inspect the AWS resources you want it to understand.

02

Ask CloudPilot

Ask questions naturally — about your bill, infrastructure, resources or something you want investigated.

03

Understand the finding

CloudPilot shows what it found, why it matters and the relevant AWS resources behind it.

04

Choose what happens next

When something should change, choose the remediation approach that fits how you work.

You stay in control

Find the problem once.
Fix it your way.

Not every team wants AI changing infrastructure automatically. CloudPilot can turn the same recommendation into four different ways to act.

01 Hands off

Automatic

Let CloudPilot perform an approved change directly for you. Best for straightforward actions you trust CloudPilot to handle.

CloudPilot → AWS → verify
02 Developer

CLI

Get the exact AWS CLI command needed to make the change yourself from your terminal.

$ aws ec2 stop-instances ...
03 Team workflow

Pull Request

Turn the recommendation into an infrastructure change your team can review through its normal code-review process.

CloudPilot → Terraform → Pull Request
04 Manual

Instructions

Prefer to do it yourself? CloudPilot can walk you through the change step by step while explaining what you're doing.

1. Open EC2 → 2. Select instance → 3. ...
No special commands
