What CloudPilot does

CloudPilot helps you understand what's actually happening in your cloud.

Instead of digging through dashboards, logs, billing pages, and configuration screens, ask CloudPilot what you want to know — and get clear answers about what's happening and what to do next.

◎

Understand what you have

CloudPilot brings together your infrastructure and the context around it, so you can understand what's running, how it fits together, and what's happening across your environment.

↓

Find waste and problems

Find unnecessary spending, risky configurations, forgotten resources, and the frustrating issues behind crashes, slowdowns, timeouts, and other problems.

✦

Help you take action

Understand what should change and why, then let CloudPilot guide the fix or safely handle supported changes for you.

How it works

Talk to your infrastructure like you would talk to a teammate.

Ask CloudPilot what you need. It investigates the systems and context behind your question, explains what it finds, and helps you decide what happens next.

Do we still need both?

From question to next step

Ask. Investigate. Understand. Act.

01

Ask

Ask CloudPilot about your infrastructure, costs, a problem you're investigating, or something you want to change.

02

Investigate

CloudPilot pulls together infrastructure, logs, configurations, history, and organizational context to figure out what's happening.

03

Understand

CloudPilot explains what it found, why it matters, and the relevant systems and context behind it.

04

Act

Decide what happens next, and let CloudPilot help you safely carry it out.

You stay in control

Find the problem once. Fix it your way.

CloudPilot gives your team control over how changes happen. Choose the approach that fits the task, your workflow, and the level of control you want.

01 · Hands off

Automatic

Let CloudPilot safely perform an approved change directly and verify the result.

CloudPilot → change → verify
02 · Developer

CLI

Prefer to make the change yourself? Get the exact command you need, with an explanation of what it will do.

$ aws ec2 stop-instances ...
03 · Team workflow

Pull Request

Turn a recommendation into an infrastructure change your team can review, approve, and deploy through its normal workflow.

CloudPilot → Terraform → Pull Request
04 · Manual

Instructions

Walk through the change step by step in the tools you already use, with CloudPilot guiding you along the way.

1. Open EC2 → 2. Select instance → 3. ...
